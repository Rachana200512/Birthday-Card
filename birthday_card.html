Dear Bro,

Now how do I write 7 years of friendship in one small letter?

I don't think I can. We've got so many memories, so many conversations, so many laughter that I don't know what to add and what not to add.

I watched a reel one day that said if your friendship survived for 7 years then it's the one you'll have for life. So, here's to being together for our entire life.. You remember during school how we used to go to each other's house for birthdays. You, me, Suvekshya, Nandani. Those days were the golden days of my life.

I miss going to each other's houses and laughing like we smelled the laughing gas somehow.

I miss going to the DADA together whenever we needed some peace. It was our go-to spot whenever we got out of the house (that Khola too).

I miss letting the fully empty magic go, just because we wanted to sit at the front. I miss how we used to take turns just so we get to sit by the window.

I miss gossiping about everything in those front seats of the magic.

I miss those restaurant huntings and the regrets we used to get when the food tasted bad. (Remember the corn dog and keema noodle of Basukala).

I miss meeting to have chatpate together.

And most of all I miss you.

But it's okay because I know you've been working really really hard there and I am super proud of you. And I know that even though we are far by god knows how many miles, we'll still be the Rachana and Shalu we were during school days.

I love you and I wish you the best in life.

Happy Birthday.

with love,
your bestfriend
